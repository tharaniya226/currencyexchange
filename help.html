Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add funds to my wallet?

Go to My Wallet, click on "Top Up" in Quick Actions, select your payment method, and enter the amount you want to add.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Navigate to Security settings, find the Two-Factor Authentication section, and click "Enable". Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the setup.

What currencies can I trade?

ForexCast supports major currency pairs including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, and more. Check the Converter page for the full list.

How accurate are the ML predictions?

Our LSTM neural network model has an accuracy rate of approximately 89.5%. However, forex markets are volatile and predictions should be used as guidance, not guarantees.

How do I withdraw funds?

Go to My Wallet, click "Withdraw" in Quick Actions, select your destination account, enter the amount, and submit. Withdrawals typically process within 1-2 business days.
